Playing Neon Eye

Neon Eye is intended for players aged 13 and older and is provided for personal entertainment. The age screen determines eligibility on your device; it does not verify your identity. Do not interfere with its services or manipulate purchases, leaderboards, saves, ads, rewards or technical protections.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The launch version offers the one-time Remove Forced Ads purchase. The iOS purchase screen shows the applicable localized price and availability. Apple handles payment, purchase restoration and refund requests through its systems.

Remove Forced Ads disables interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available when you choose to watch for an in-game reward. A refunded or revoked entitlement may no longer provide access.

Ads, rewards and online services

Ad availability depends on network, region, consent and third-party services. A video that fails or is closed before completion does not grant a continue. A rewarded continue makes that run unranked.

Game Center, purchases, Restore and ads depend on online services and applicable account or platform rules. Their availability is not guaranteed. Gameplay saves and Personal Records are local; Restore Purchases does not restore game progress.
