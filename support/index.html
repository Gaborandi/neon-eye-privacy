What To Include

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment card details.

Use Restore Purchases inside Neon Eye. If restore does not work, include the product you purchased and the Apple account region used for the purchase.

For privacy access, correction, deletion, or consent-choice questions, include enough information to identify the relevant app-generated player record. Apple purchase history and Game Center account data must also be managed through Apple's account tools.
