Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Last updated: October 4, 2026 · English notice

This notice describes the Neon Eye launch version and this website. Neon Eye is an independent game by Gaborandi.

Gameplay and local data

Campaign and Flux saves, Personal Records, medals, Daily history, eye skins and settings are stored on your device. Purchase entitlement and delivery state are also kept locally so the app can apply verified access correctly.

This version has no custom cloud-save or remote leaderboard backend. The game's own gameplay-analytics delivery is disabled in this launch configuration. That does not mean the Apple and Google services described below collect no data.

Apple and Google services

Advertising and consent

Neon Eye integrates Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent and privacy choices where required. Depending on consent, device settings and service operation, Google may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad and app interactions, crash reports, performance information and other diagnostics. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement, analytics and service operation. The app can support personalized ads when the required consent and Apple tracking permission are both granted. Where permitted, Google can use identifiers to link data with information from other companies' apps or websites for advertising and measurement.

Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and Google's consent choices serve different purposes. Refusing Apple's tracking permission does not remove all ads or all SDK data processing. Non-personalized ads are not the same as no data collection.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases and Game Center

Apple processes payments and purchase records. Neon Eye uses StoreKit to verify the Remove Forced Ads entitlement; the app does not receive your payment-card details. Where available, Game Center handles authentication and supported leaderboard scores. Local Personal Records are separate from Game Center.

Apple handles its account and service data under Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

Use iOS Settings to manage tracking permission. Neon Eye Settings shows Ad Privacy Choices when the consent service requires that option. You can revisit the available privacy choices there.

Settings → Delete Local Data removes local gameplay progress, settings and Personal Records after confirmation. It does not delete Apple purchase history, cancel a purchase, request a refund or erase data held by Apple or Google. The app preserves local purchase-entitlement state during this reset; verified purchase access can also be restored through Apple. Deleting local progress is irreversible in the game.

Local gameplay data remains until you delete it or remove the app, subject to the device's storage and backup behavior. There is no Neon Eye cloud-save recovery service. Apple and Google apply their own retention and deletion policies to their data.

This website and support

This site stores your navigation-language preference in browser storage. It does not include advertising scripts, analytics scripts or a contact form. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security. The host handles that information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Voluntary support

If you contact Neon Eye support, we use the contact details and information you choose to provide to answer your request. Start with a short description, the app version and device/OS information. Do not send passwords, verification codes, Apple credentials, payment-card details, your date of birth or a complete saved game. We request a cropped screenshot or limited diagnostic only when needed; please hide unrelated personal information.

We do not use support details for advertising, audience matching or a mailing list. Under the support process adopted for launch, attachments will be deleted when the issue is resolved, and correspondence will be deleted within 90 days after resolution, unless a specific legal requirement or unresolved dispute requires longer retention. You can request earlier deletion using the support address.

Apple, Google and the email/hosting providers handle their independently controlled data under their own policies. Deleting data through Neon Eye support does not erase those providers' records or backups.

Age and privacy requests

Neon Eye is intended for players aged 13 and older. Your date of birth is checked on your device to determine eligibility, then discarded; the app retains the eligibility result. This is not identity verification. Players below the minimum age cannot enter the game.

For questions or requests about access, correction, deletion or privacy choices, use the existing support contact. Describe the request without sending unnecessary sensitive information. Requests concerning data held by Apple or Google may also need to go through those providers.
