# Neon Eye Policy Site

This repository publishes the public policy pages for Neon Eye.

Live URLs:

- Privacy Policy: `https://gaborandi.github.io/neon-eye-privacy/`
- Privacy redirect: `https://gaborandi.github.io/neon-eye-privacy/privacy/`
- Terms: `https://gaborandi.github.io/neon-eye-privacy/terms/`
- Support: `https://gaborandi.github.io/neon-eye-privacy/support/`

The May 7, 2026 policy reflects the current monetized launch profile: Google
AdMob, Google UMP consent, App Tracking Transparency, analytics, Apple Game
Center, Apple StoreKit purchases, Supabase leaderboards/cloud save, receipt
validation, Remove Ads, limited/non-personalized ads, support, and deletion
requests.

The site uses Neon Eye visual styling, a Rally-style public site shell, and a
language selector for English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese,
Italian, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. The full English policy
remains the authoritative version.
