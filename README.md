Live URLs:

The May 7, 2026 policy reflects the current monetized launch profile: Google
AdMob, Google UMP consent, App Tracking Transparency, analytics, Apple Game
Center, Apple StoreKit purchases, Supabase leaderboards/cloud save, receipt
validation, Remove Ads, limited/non-personalized ads, support, and deletion
requests.
