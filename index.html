Privacy Policy

Neon Eye — Last Updated: March 6, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy describes how Neon Eye ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you use the app, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, screen resolution, and locale
  • Usage Data: Gameplay patterns (stages completed, scores, session duration, game mode played)
  • Ad Interaction Data: Views and interactions with advertisements (with your consent)
  • Performance Data: Frame rate, latency measurements, and crash reports for improving app stability

Information You Provide

  • In-App Purchases: Transaction information is processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit and is not stored by us
  • Game Center: If you sign into Game Center, your Game Center display name and scores are shared with Apple's leaderboard service

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • We do not collect photos, contacts, or location data
  • We do not collect financial or payment information (Apple handles all purchases)

How We Use Your Information

  • Improve the App: Analyze gameplay data to optimize difficulty balance, fix bugs, and enhance performance
  • Show Advertisements: Display ads to support the free app (personalized ads require your explicit consent via App Tracking Transparency)
  • Leaderboards: Display scores on in-game and Game Center leaderboards
  • Analytics: Understand aggregate usage patterns to improve the game experience

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad targeting, subject to your consent. Google Privacy Policy

Apple Game Center

Leaderboard functionality is powered by Apple Game Center. Your Game Center profile and scores are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple StoreKit

All in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's Privacy Policy

Supabase

We use Supabase for cloud-based leaderboard storage. Only anonymized player identifiers and game scores are transmitted. No personally identifiable information is sent to Supabase.

Your Choices

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 and later, we request your permission before tracking your activity for personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Neon Eye.

GDPR Consent

If you are located in the EU or UK, we will ask for your consent before collecting analytics data. You may decline, and the game will still be fully playable.

Remove Ads

You can purchase "Remove Ads" ($2.99) to eliminate interstitial advertisements and reduce ad-related data collection. Optional rewarded video ads remain available as a bonus feature you can choose to watch.

Data Storage & Retention

  • Gameplay progress: Stored locally on your device (localStorage). We do not have access to this data.
  • Leaderboard scores: Stored on our Supabase server with anonymized identifiers only
  • Purchase receipts: Managed by Apple per their retention policies
  • Ad data: Managed by Google AdMob per their retention policies
  • Analytics events: Aggregated and anonymized; individual events are not retained beyond 90 days

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Apple: For app distribution, in-app purchases, and Game Center
  • Google (AdMob): For ad delivery (with your consent)
  • Supabase: For anonymized leaderboard data only

Children's Privacy

Neon Eye includes an age gate requiring users to confirm they are 13 years or older before accessing the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of data collection and ad tracking
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable privacy laws including:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU/UK users
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
