Neon Eye Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Privacy Terms Support

This policy explains how Neon Eye uses data in the mobile timing game. The current launch profile includes ads, Google UMP consent, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, analytics, Apple Game Center, Apple StoreKit purchases, Supabase leaderboards, Supabase cloud save, and receipt-related services where configured.

Information We Collect

Gameplay And App Usage

We may process stages played, scores, medals, session duration, selected game mode, daily challenge state, cosmetic/progression state, settings, ad/revive interactions, shop interactions, technical errors, diagnostics, and performance data. This is used to operate the game, tune difficulty, diagnose issues, protect against abuse, and measure game health.

Online Features

When online features are available, Neon Eye may process leaderboard entries, cloud-save records, app-generated user identifiers, Game Center identifiers, scores, stage progress, purchase entitlement state, and related timestamps through Supabase and Apple services.

Advertising And Tracking

Neon Eye uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect advertising data, device identifiers, ad interactions, and measurement data according to Google's policies. On iOS, tracking that requires permission is controlled by Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit. We store entitlement state needed to unlock purchases such as Remove Ads, Battle Pass, Starter Bundle, and coin packs. Apple processes payment information; we do not receive full payment card details.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, videos, or precise location.
  • We do not receive your payment card number from Apple.

How We Use Information

  • Provide gameplay, local save, cloud save, leaderboards, and progression.
  • Operate purchases, purchase restoration, and entitlement checks.
  • Show, cap, measure, and troubleshoot ads, subject to consent and platform settings.
  • Improve balance, stability, performance, fraud prevention, and abuse protection.
  • Respond to support, privacy, deletion, and compliance requests.

Third-Party Services

Google Mobile Ads And Google UMP

Google Mobile Ads provides ads. Google User Messaging Platform manages consent for applicable regions and may expose privacy choices in the app. If consent is limited, Neon Eye requests limited or non-personalized ads where applicable.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad Settings

Apple StoreKit, Game Center, And App Tracking Transparency

Apple provides purchase processing, purchase restoration, Game Center leaderboard identity and scores, and the system tracking permission prompt.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

Supabase

Supabase may be used for analytics ingestion, cloud save, leaderboard services, and receipt validation. Data stored there is used to operate, protect, and improve the game.

  • Supabase Privacy Policy

Your Choices

Tracking Permission

On iOS, you can allow or deny tracking when prompted. You can change this choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Neon Eye.

Ad Consent And Privacy Choices

Where required, Google UMP may show a consent form. When Google reports that privacy options are required, the app settings can show an Ad Privacy Choices entry point.

Remove Ads

The Remove Ads purchase disables interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available because they are initiated by the player for a reward.

Support And Deletion Requests

To request help, access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact us at the email below. Some data, such as Apple purchase history and Game Center account data, is controlled by Apple and must be managed through Apple.

Data Retention

We retain gameplay, analytics, leaderboard, cloud-save, and entitlement data as long as needed to operate the game, maintain records, prevent abuse, and comply with legal requirements. Third-party services retain data according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Neon Eye is not directed to children under 13. The app includes age/consent guardrails where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared with service providers and platform partners only as needed to operate the game:

  • Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, Game Center, and platform privacy controls.
  • Google for ads, consent, and ad measurement.
  • Supabase for analytics, cloud save, leaderboards, and receipt validation.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The updated policy will include a new Last updated date.
